Questions, problems or deletion requests

Email meric1357@icloud.com. Please include your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > LearnKit.

I bought a subscription on another device. Open LearnKit and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen, or in Settings.

A summary failed. Some pages block automated access and some files have no readable text (for example scanned PDFs with very low quality). Try another source or a clearer photo, then tap Retry.
